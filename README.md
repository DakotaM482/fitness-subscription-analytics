This is an analysis of the fitness_subscriptions_dataset to learn customer cohort and to make predictions based off of prior data. The average customer will leave within the first 4-5 months. While customers do continue to steadily fall off after this point it is not as drastic. So, figuring out ways to keep customers during this time is crucial. The forecast has a predicted revenue of $305,065.00 by November 2026. There is no clear seasonality shown as revenue has been on a steady increase over the past 2 years. In 2024 it took until June to recover their customer acquisition cost while in 2025 it took until July. The company shows steady growth however if we can find ways to better encourage customers to stay past the first few months, we would be able to retain much more revenue.
